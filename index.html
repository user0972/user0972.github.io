What is Brickadia

WHAT IS Brickadia?

Brickadia is the best place to Imagine with Friends™. With the largest user-generated online gaming platform, and over 15 million games created by users, Brickadia is the #1 gaming site for kids and teens (comScore). Every day, virtual explorers come to Brickadia to create adventures, play games, role play, and learn with their friends in a family-friendly, immersive, 3D environment.

Brickadia on your device

Brickadia ON YOUR DEVICE

You can access Brickadia on PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Amazon Devices, and Xbox One. Brickadia adventures are accessible from any device, so players can imagine with their friends regardless of where they are.
